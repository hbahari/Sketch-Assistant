# Privacy Policy — Sketch Assistant

**Last updated: 26 October 2026**

Sketch Assistant turns a photo into a pencil sketch and a step-by-step drawing guide.
Everything it does happens on your device. The app has no accounts, no servers and no
analytics, and it does not upload your photos or any other information anywhere.

## Photos you choose

When you tap **Add a photo → Choose from gallery**, Android's own photo picker opens.
The app does not browse your photo library and does not ask for storage permission: you
pick one image, and only that image is handed to the app.

That image is copied into the app's private storage so it can be worked on, then analysed
on the device to find faces and to draw the sketch. The working copy is replaced the next
time you choose a photo, and is removed if you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Your photos are never uploaded, never shared, and never seen by the developer.

## Camera

When you tap **Add a photo → Take photo**, your phone's own camera app opens and takes the
picture. Sketch Assistant does not hold the camera permission and has no direct access to
the camera. The resulting photo is handled exactly like one chosen from the gallery.

## Sketches you save

Tapping **Save PNG** writes the finished sketch into your device's gallery, in a folder
called **Pictures/SketchAssistant**. These files stay on your device and belong to you.
The app does not read them back, index them, or send them anywhere.

## Face detection

Finding faces in a photo is done entirely on the device, by a small detection model
included inside the app. No face data, measurement or image ever leaves your phone, and
nothing is stored: the detection result exists only while the photo is open in the app.

The app does not identify or recognise anyone. It only locates where a face sits in the
picture so it can crop and sketch it.

## Other data

Sketch Assistant contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising networks and no trackers. It
shows no ads. It does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, device
identifiers or any other personal information, and it does not create an account or ask
you to sign in.

The app does not make network requests. It works fully offline, including in aeroplane mode.

## Children

Sketch Assistant knowingly collects no personal information from anyone, including
children. No data leaves the device, so there is nothing to collect, store or share.

## Changes to this policy

This policy would be updated here if a future version of the app added accounts, cloud
sync, purchases, analytics or anything else that changed how information is handled. The
date at the top shows when the policy last changed.
